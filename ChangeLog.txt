# Down Under Gambler's Sweat It Out! Derby '27 - Change Log

## Version 0.5.0 (2026-11-24)

The Shady Koala, where most of the strategy is, after 5-player playtests: its two attacks
are now a gamble, and everyone gets to see what happened (but never who paid for it).

### New Features
- **Have an Accident** (replaces Bat to Kneecaps, same 15% price): the target takes a fall
  (-2 SPEED and STRENGTH), gets run over by a car (-4) or takes a bat to the kneecaps (-6),
  equal odds
- **Juice** (replaces HGH Injection, same 10% price): a cup of joe (+2 SPEED and
  STRENGTH), HGH (+4) or sand lobster venom (+6), equal odds
- **Breaking news**: before the event, every accident that got through flashes up on the
  big screen as a breaking news headline with the player's head ("Davo got hit by a
  car!"). A player who's had the same accident before this season gets "[again]"
- **Speech balloons**: when the event screen opens, a cartoon speech balloon pops up for
  every juice ("Shazza is looking jacked.") before the starting gun
- Effects on the same player add up, and SPEED and STRENGTH never go below 0
- **Strength rebalanced**: an all-STRENGTH character won Fist Fight, Sand Curling and Sumo
  every time, which left three events per season with the same odds. Fist Fight is now
  STR x 1.5 - SPD - MCR + LUCK, Sand Curling STR x 1.5 + SPD x 1.2 + LUCK, and Sumo
  STR x 2 + MCR x 1.5 + LUCK (rounded to whole numbers, halves up). Strength still owns Fist
  Fight; a speed build now takes about a third of Sand Curlings and a moral courage build a
  tenth of Sumos. Strength keeps a slight edge overall

### Packaging / Internal
- tests/balance.py: pits skill builds against each other over thousands of simulated
  seasons (no Shady Koala, no betting) and prints each build's win rate per event. Try
  formula changes with --proposed and wider LUCK with --luck-high
- rules.KOALA_OUTCOMES, roll_koala, koala_headlines, news_seconds, balloon_seconds;
  effective_stats takes rolled outcomes. Item ids are now accident / juice / bribe
- New `news` phase between frisk and perform (skipped with no accidents); the perform
  timer adds the balloon intro, and performance has `intro` and `balloons`
- Developer Mode samples: breaking news (first headline, every headline), juice balloons,
  and the phone during the news
- Tests: outcome odds, stacking and the 0 floor, [again] across events and new games,
  the news flow and that it never names the buyer; the browser test buys an accident
  and juice and checks the news and balloon screens; the new formulas and their rounding

## Version 0.4.0 (2026-11-24)

More catch-up for whoever is behind, so the leader can't run away with the season, and
bigger specials and loans to match seasons that now end in the tens of thousands.

### Bug Fixes
- **Fixed "phantom" players left over from name changes** (HIGH)
  - Issue: players who re-joined under a new name (a fresh tab, a lost session) left their
    old player behind, greyed out; it was carried into the game with a blank character and
    long odds
  - Root cause: APGE matched players by name only, so a new name was always a new player
  - Solution: APGE 0.4.0 drops those leftovers in the lobby (same phone, now playing as
    someone else). Sleeping phones are never dropped
  - Files modified: requirements.txt (APGE 0.4.0), game.py

### New Features
- **Change name**: a "Change name" button in the phone's header in the lobby (including
  while drawing your head)
- **Mother's Purse pays more**: a lone thief takes the whole $Dd 1,000; if nobody steals,
  everyone gets $Dd 200; if more than one player steals, everyone loses 25% of their
  cash. The TV only says that everyone lost a quarter, since the amounts would reveal
  everyone's cash; each phone shows its own loss
- **Case or No Case raised**: the case holds $Dd 500-10,000 (players are never told the
  range). Take the CASE and the voters split it while the holder gets $Dd 250; take the
  CASH and every voter gets $Dd 250 while the holder keeps the case
- **Ticket scalping raised**: face values of $Dd 150-1,500, with $Dd 10 / 100 step buttons
  for your asking price
- **Loansharks are for going big**: always on offer, a fixed $Dd 5,000 (you'll owe
  $Dd 10,000), take it or leave it, once per betting event
- **Shady Koala visits by standings**: the player in 1st place never gets a visit, last
  place always does, and everyone else has a 50% chance. This replaces the per-league
  visit counts. Players tied for a place share it; at the first event everyone is level,
  so everyone gets the 50% chance
- **Shady Koala prices scale with your cash**: Bat to Kneecaps costs 15%, HGH Injection
  10% and Bribe the Feds 5% of the cash you hold when you open the menu (at least
  $Dd 1). The menu shows the dollar amounts
- **Card auction is 30 seconds** (was 60)

### Packaging / Internal
- rules.koala_visitors and rules.koala_prices; ZONES no longer has koala_visits
- Built on APGE 0.4.0 (leftover players dropped, rename, on_player_removed hook)
- rules.LOAN_AMOUNT replaces LOAN_MIN; purse_payouts takes everyone's cash; the borrow
  action ignores any amount sent
- Tests: visits by standings and ties, percent prices fixed for the visit; the browser
  test sets the first event's standings so the visits are predictable

## Version 0.3.0 (2026-11-24)

Tweaks from the first 2 and 3 player playtests, where 99-1 longshots plus a few
well-placed kneecaps turned two players' cash into tens of thousands and left everyone
else's totals meaningless.

### Bug Fixes
- **Fixed the Sand Curling animation sometimes not appearing** (HIGH)
  - Issue: some Sand Curling events showed an empty stage on the TV; other times it played.
    Mini Golf and Half-Court Shots could do the same
  - Root cause: the browser's first animation frame can be timestamped a few milliseconds
    before the moment the scene started (about 1 frame in 6 in Chromium), so the scene saw
    a slightly negative time. The turn-by-turn animation turned that into turn number -1,
    threw an error, and the stage dropped the scene for the rest of the event
  - Solution: animation time never goes below zero, the turn number is clamped, and a scene
    that throws keeps drawing on the next frame instead of being dropped
  - Files modified: static/stage.js
- **Fixed phones spoiling the final results** (MEDIUM)
  - Issue: every phone showed its place and final total the moment the season ended,
    while the TV was still revealing the standings
  - Root cause: the phone's final screen rendered as soon as game_over began; only the
    host paced the reveal
  - Solution: phones say "the final results are on the big screen" until the TV announces
    the winner (after the loansharks collect), using the same pacing as the host
  - Files modified: static/player.js, static/host.js, static/common.js

### New Features
- **Longest odds are now 50-1** (were 99-1)
- **Event winner's cut**: the player who wins an event takes 10% of the winnings on every
  winning bet, out of the bettors' payouts (bets on yourself included). The TV payout
  screen and the winner's phone show the cut, and the phone's "On win" preview includes it
- **Shuffled season**: the events and the special events come in a random order every
  game (including One more game). The season keeps its shape: 13 stops, the specials in
  the same slots, and the same three zones. More events can be added to the pools later,
  so some will sit out each season
- **Bet in 1s, 10s or 100s**: step buttons under the betting slip's counters (and the
  card auction's bid) set how much each +/- moves. Bets and bids can be any whole amount
- **Your bets during the event**: from locking in through the animation, the phone lists
  your bets (with what each would pay) and your Shady Koala purchases (marked confiscated
  if you were frisked). The betting slip also lists what you've bought from the Koala
- **Bigger host timer** that turns red and beeps once at 10 seconds left, whenever players
  are choosing or pressing Continue. Browsers only allow sound after the host page has
  been clicked once, so the header asks for a click until then
- **Card pack worth 10x**: the five cards are worth $Dd 10-100 each and the holographic
  card $Dd 50-2000. Both bidding screens show the pack's possible value ($Dd 100 - $Dd 2,500)
- Money shows thousands separators ($Dd 12,500)

### Packaging / Internal
- Uses APGE 0.3.0's new countdown warnings (`bindCountdown` urgentAt/onUrgent) and
  `APGE.sound.beep()`, added for this
- rules.build_season (pools GAMBLE_STOPS / SPECIALS, SEASON_SHAPE) replaces rules.SEASON;
  sample screens and the browser test use the unshuffled route
- New Developer Mode samples: betting slip after the Shady Koala, bets locked in, phone
  during the event with bets, phone final results before and after the reveal
- Tests: odds ceiling, winner's cut, shuffled seasons, any-amount bets and bids, a winner
  kicked before payout; the browser test also checks the step buttons, the phone's bets
  during the race, the timer warning and single beep, a negative-time animation frame and
  the held-back final place

## Version 0.2.0 (2026-11-22)

### New Features
- **Developer Mode** (`python app.py --dev`, opens /dev): the art CMS and a screen gallery,
  separate from playing. Every picture the game needs is listed by category (29 of them:
  title, characters, props, the nine venues, event scenery, the map), each with its own
  canvas size (Agent Kevin is 32x48, tall and narrow). Draw, Save, Save As (new version, or
  a starting point for other art), import PNGs, and pick the version used in game. The
  live preview shows the drawing on the real screens it appears on before it's saved
- **Screens tab**: all 45 host and phone screens with four sample players, from the lobby
  to the final reveal, for checking art and layout without playing to that point
- Art drawn in Developer Mode replaces its placeholder box on the TV and phones; anything
  not drawn yet stays an `[ART: ...]` box
- The head-drawing pad gained a colour picker tool

### Packaging / Internal
- Built on APGE 0.3.0 (Developer Mode, art library, shared pixel editor - all added for
  this game). The pixel editor now comes from APGE; /art and static/pixelart.js are gone
- dev.py: the art list and sample screens; venue art ids from rules.venue_art()
- Tests: tests/test_dev.py (every sample screen builds, art list matches the JS) and
  tests/test_dev_browser.py (Playwright drives Developer Mode and loads every screen)

## Version 0.1.0 (2026-11-15)

First playable draft of the design doc's critical path, with placeholder art.

### New Features
- **Character creation**: join with a name (15 letters), draw a 16x16 pixel head on the
  phone (pencil, eraser, fill, mirror, undo), spend 10 skill points on STR / SPD / MCR.
  The first player to finish is the HOST and starts the game from their phone
- **Season**: 13 stops across NZ and Australia in three zones, with a map between stops
- **Gambling events**: Foot Race, Speed Hiking, Fist Fight, Australian Rules Sand Curling,
  Mini Golf, Mascot Race, Sumo Wrestle, Half-Court Shots, Holding A Note. Odds are simulated
  from everyone's stats, bets go up in $Dd 10 steps with a live "On win" preview, and each
  event has a host-screen animation (race lanes, last-one-standing, or turn by turn)
- **Shady Koala**: secret visits (more per zone) selling Bat to Kneecaps, HGH Injection and
  Bribe the Feds. **Kangarules Gambling Commission** frisks negate everything a frisked
  player bought and show it on the big screen
- **Loansharks**: Shrimp and Barbie lend to anyone below the starting cash; they want double
  back at the end
- **Special events**: Mother's Purse, Trading Card Auction, Case or No Case, Ticket Scalping
- **Final results**: standings revealed last place first, then the loans are collected, then
  the winner and awards. The host can start one more game or quit
- **Art tool** at /art: the same pixel editor at 16-64 px, saves PNG and editable .txt

### Packaging / Internal
- Built on APGE 0.2.0 (VIP phone controls, quit, per-game name length, lobby setup in the
  bot harness - all added for this game)
- Tests: rules unit tests, bot matrix (4 bots, every standard scenario, ghosts quitting in
  every input phase), Playwright browser check of every screen
